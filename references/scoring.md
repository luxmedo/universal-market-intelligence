# Opportunity scoring

Score each dimension 0–5 if evidence exists:
1. Demand evidence
2. Trend stability
3. Customer pain / willingness to pay
4. Accessible acquisition channels
5. Gross margin potential
6. Differentiation
7. Legal and platform feasibility
8. Operational feasibility

Total /40 when all dimensions are scored.
0–15: weak; 16–27: investigate; 28–40: promising for a controlled test.
Use N/A for unknowns and state coverage (scored dimensions / 8). Do not award high scores merely because evidence is missing.
Provide conservative, base and optimistic assumptions separately.
